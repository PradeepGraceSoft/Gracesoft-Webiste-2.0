Your Questions, Answered

What is hospitality property management software?

A hospitality property management system (PMS) helps lodging teams organize reservations, room availability, guest records, billing and daily operations.

What is GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping?

Easy InnKeeping is GraceSoft’s cloud-based hospitality PMS, with connected booking, distribution, guest engagement and operational tools.

What types of properties does GraceSoft support?

GraceSoft serves independent hotels, motels, bed and breakfasts, inns, resorts, vacation rentals, campgrounds, RV parks, dude ranches, condos and multi-property businesses.

Does GraceSoft provide an online booking engine?

Yes. GraceSoft offers an online booking engine that connects with property websites so guests can check availability and reserve directly.

Does GraceSoft support OTA integrations?

Yes. GraceSoft lists connections with online travel agencies (OTAs) and booking platforms, including Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb and Vrbo. Confirm current availability for your setup.

Can GraceSoft manage multiple properties?

Yes. GraceSoft offers multi-property management, including access to multiple properties through a unified login. Discuss portfolio requirements with the team.

Does GraceSoft offer AI Voice and Chat Assistants?

Yes. GraceSoft describes AI voice and chat options for property questions, availability, rates and booking inquiries, including outside normal desk hours.

What payment gateways does GraceSoft support?

Published options include Stripe, Square, Authorize.Net and Shift4. Gateway support varies by location and configuration; check the payments page and confirm with GraceSoft.

Can guests book directly through a property’s website?

Yes. The online booking engine provides a direct reservation path from your website. GraceSoft also offers website solutions for lodging businesses.

Does GraceSoft provide mobile access?

Yes. GraceSoft describes cloud access from desktop, tablet and mobile, with apps for Android, iPhone and iPad. Confirm the workflows available on your device.

How can I request a GraceSoft product demonstration?

Use Request a Demo to discuss your property, team and workflows. You can also review pricing or contact GraceSoft before choosing a configuration.
