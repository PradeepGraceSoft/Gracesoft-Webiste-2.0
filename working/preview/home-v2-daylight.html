What is GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping?

GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping is hospitality property management software designed to help lodging operators manage reservations, front desk workflows, room availability and guest communication. Its connected product offering also covers online bookings, channel distribution and other operational needs. Features and integrations depend on plan and configuration.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about Easy InnKeeping

What is hotel property management software?

A property management system (PMS) is software lodging teams use to organize operational work such as reservations, room availability and front desk workflows. Specific tools vary by provider and configuration.

Which kinds of properties can explore Easy InnKeeping?

GraceSoft presents solutions for hotels, bed and breakfasts, vacation rentals, dude ranches, condominiums and campgrounds or RV parks. Visit the relevant solution page to review fit for your property.

Can guests book through my property's website?

GraceSoft offers an online booking engine. Review its current capabilities and requirements with the team for your website and account configuration.

Does the software connect to booking channels?

A channel manager is part of GraceSoft's product offering. Supported channels and configuration can change, so confirm the current options directly with GraceSoft.

Can I manage guest details and reservations together?

GraceSoft describes property management and CRM tools for lodging workflows. Ask which functions and data are included in the plan and setup you are considering.

How can I understand pricing and product fit?

Review the pricing page and request a demo to discuss your property type, workflows and configuration.
