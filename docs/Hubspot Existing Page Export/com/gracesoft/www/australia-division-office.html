Gracesoft Transcending its Territories

Gracesoft is proud to widen its horizon for introducing its Easy Innkeeping in Australia which has a tremendous potential for Hotel industry. Tourism is one of the predominant avenues in Australia that contributes to its economy and the hospitality industry being the largest to employ human resources. Over 6,000 Australian hotels generate between $11 billion and $14 billion per year in domestic economic activity. Taking advantage of the viability of this industry, Gracesoft proposes to enable such industries to manage their property through the exclusive software ‘Easy Inn Keeping’ in Australia for the Hotels to develop and maintain the revenue, that are directly generated through tourism.

Easy Inn Keeping is a feature rich cloud based system that integrates property management system, web reservations with online booking engine and a complete solution to the point of sale. It is a user-friendly “Easy to use” tool for all reservation bookings and management which is used by more than 1000+ clients and also one of the Gracesoft partners in Australia.

Easy Inn Keeping is compatible to work with iPad, iPhone and with any smartphone which attracts more clients all over the world and with our partners. Though Gracesoft has been making a leap-forward growth for the past 15 years in North and South America, Europe, Middle East,  Asia, it just flocking its feathers in Australia for the growing need of the nation.

The rapid and sustained economic recovery in Australia, aided by augment resources sector, is driving renewed demand and record occupancy levels which enables our product to be a valued one.
