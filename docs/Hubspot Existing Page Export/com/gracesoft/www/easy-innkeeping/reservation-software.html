Easy InnKeeping ™ Web Based Reservation Software

The Complete B&B Software and Reservation System

This reservation software is well suited for small to medium sized inns, hotels, cabins, cottages, Bed and Breakfasts, and campgrounds. The Easy InnKeeping™ system helps you manage online reservations, guest profiles, and credit card payments. The software was first tested and used by group of small inns and B&B in Lanesboro, MN, USA. After successfully testing the product, for ease of use, the product was later developed to fit larger properties. Now Easy Innkeeping has become a great option for anyone needing instant access via mobile phones or any ipad or tablet to reservation data or availability.  Use a variety of reports to help analyze marketing and financial results. The following modules all interface seamlessly with Easy InnKeeping.
- Credit Card processing
- Sales & Marketing Module  , e-mail and marketing correspondence
- Easy WebRez  Online Reservation  System
- GDS  (global distribution system). Sell rooms through popular online travel sites and travel agents
- Google Analytics connects to your reservation software to bring the power for analytics to your booking engine.

 

The System where you Manage Reservations on One Screen

  1. In Easy InnKeeping, the colorful availability charts are your 'dashboard' for managing reservations!
  2. The reservation form opens when you click on the room and arrival date
  3. Quick Reservation provides a one-screen entry for guest info, dates, room, rate, discounts and payments.
  4. Print a bill, send a letter, or check-in guests with "one click" using the quick link menu.

Inn and B&B Reservation Software with Global Marketing Impact!

In today's economy, bed and breakfast software or inn software should maximize your marketing potential. Through partnership with InnLink, Easy InnKeeping becomes your portal for selling rooms to thousands of online booking sites that includes Travelocity, Hotwire and Orbitz along with 250,000 travel agents globally.

Through the GDS interface, you can:

- Market your rooms to a global market for those slower, hard-to-fill dates and seasons
- Block your busy weekends, and avoid giving online discount prices during your peak times
- Gain new market share and raise visibility for your property!
- GDS bookings are all confirmed with a credit card and download to your Easy InnKeeping reservation calendar
