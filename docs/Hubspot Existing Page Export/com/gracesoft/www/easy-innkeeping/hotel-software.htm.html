Easy InnKeeping Hotel Software

Easy InnKeeping Motel, condo, resort and hotel software. Designed to help manage reservations, Easy InnKeeping interfaces with an online reservation system for web site bookings, GDS and is compatible with mobile devices. This hotel software provides you with the option for assigning different security levels for your staff, night audit, and reports for analyzing marketing and financial data.

The "One Stop Shop" for Hotel Software

Gracesoft provides an Easy to Use solutions that are Comprehensive and Accessible from anywhere. The Hotel software is a hospitality suite that is a "One Stop Shop" where you can manage Multiple Properties, Electronic Interfaces, Website, Booking Engine, Email Marketing, gift shop POS, Restaurant POS, Direct Connects with OTA'S, GDS Connectivity all from your Hotel Management Software in one easy to use product. It is ideal for hotels,motels that need multiple interfaces with various 3rd party systems.You will love the way our hotel software integrates all these critical operations into one platform with one login.

Easy Innkeeping Hotel Software Interfaces with these Electronic Devices

Connect with multiple electronic interfaces to consolidate your functions into one system.  You can easily capture signature, record copies of ID's, and swipe credit cards.

Electronic Signatures

Save paper, ink, and filing time with our interface to capture electronic signatures.  You can have the guest sign for the charges, policies, or even sign a large document.  The signed document will then be recorded in the history tab of the reservation.

ID Scanner

Scan the Drivers License or Passport direction into the guest record and have it stored for future reservations.

Credit Card Swipe Device

Swipe the credit card number directly into the software and have it stored for future use.  Then process the payment through our system; with a connection a payment Gateway through Authorize.net or PayPal.  No more double entry.

Easy WebPOS (Restaurant/Gift Shop)

Connect your POS system directly with your hotel software. Guest charges easily transfer from the restaurant or gift shop directly to the guest folio.  Gain access to all your PMS and POS reports in one location. Read more..

POS Interface

Touch Screen interface that works great on a computer or tablet allowing you to connect from any location at the property.  Gain easy access as an owner/manager from any location to stay on top of the activity in your POS system.  You can quickly push a charge to the guest room, add a promo code or discount, track your staff, and communicate between the waiter/waitress to the kitchen.

Stock Management Interface

Track your incoming stock/purchases and easily review what you need to order and where you have a surplice that needs to be promoted to your guest.

The Hotel Reservation software that is Easy to Use!

In comparison with other software for hotels, we pride ourselves on our products’ easy-to-use interface. Our product was names as the "easiest to use" hotel software by Inn Sights magazine. You will find yourself spending less time learning our software and training staff; and more time enjoying the benefits of using Easy InnKeeping. With our free online training services, your management and staff will be effective in using your hotel software to manage guests, housekeeping and reservations.

Free Online Training includes the following:

Setup Training & Installation: Setup rooms, rates, taxes, amenities etc.
Front Desk Activities: Make reservations, print and email confirmation letters, check-in guests, check-out guests, print receipts.
Back Office and staff management: Create staff logins, export reports to Excel, run night audit.
Online Reservations: Review set up photos, images and define policies for online bookings.
GDS Setup and Training:  Once Easy InnKeeping’s room and rate setup is complete, data will be loaded into the GDS system then training and review of the data is scheduled with the client. Upon approval, the GDS will be put online.
