Frequently Asked Questions

How much does GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping cost per month?

Pricing varies based on your property size, number of rooms, and required features. GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping provides customized pricing to ensure you only pay for what your business needs. 

Does GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping charge commission fees?

No. GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping helps properties increase direct bookings without charging extra commission on reservations made through your own website.

Can I upgrade my pricing plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade your plan as your property grows or when you need more advanced features. 

Is support included in the pricing?

Yes. All plans include customer support, with advanced support options available in higher-tier plans.
