Reservation Confirmation

Check-In Information

Send arrival details, check-in instructions, room information, and other important information guests need before reaching the property.

Check-Out Notifications

Send check-out reminders and relevant departure information to help make the end of the guest stay more convenient.

Automated Guest Messages

Two-Way Guest Communication

Promotional Messages

Promote special offers, packages, loyalty incentives, and other hotel promotions to guests through WhatsApp, where appropriate and with the required guest consent.

Post-Stay Communication

Stay connected after departure with thank-you messages, review requests, and relevant offers designed to encourage repeat bookings and continued guest engagement.
