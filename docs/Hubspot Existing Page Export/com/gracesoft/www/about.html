Our Story

Founded in 1999, GraceSoft has provided innovative property management software solutions to hospitality businesses for more than 25 years. 

GraceSoft was founded on the belief that powerful technology should be made accessible to everyone through innovative products. Our core value is simple: we provide tools that are easy to learn, easy to use, and easy on the price, allowing you to focus on what matters most and not worry about the technology. 

We’ve seen "easy" become a common industry buzzword, but at GraceSoft, it remains our North Star. We were among the first to champion this concept long before it was a trend, and we have never wavered from our founding principle: delivering powerful software that is effortless to master, simple to operate, and affordable for every owner. 

From our early days being recognized for ease of use by InnSights Magazine to our current global standing, we have stayed the course. Despite the increasing complexities of modern technology, we work tirelessly to strip away the noise. This "Easy-to-Use" mindset isn't just a marketing slogan—it is the standard every department in our organization follows to ensure your success. 

In 2005, GraceSoft launched its first cloud-based hospitality software GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping, allowing property owners to manage reservations and operations securely from anywhere. 

Today, GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping continues to evolve by listening to customers and adapting to hospitality technology trends worldwide. 

Over the years, GraceSoft has distinguished itself by providing easy-to-use solutions for the independent inn, hotel, or condo owner. GraceSoft launched its first cloud-based product for this market in 2005. Our strategy is to continually innovate by listening to our customer base and staying abreast of market and technology trends.

Our Mission

Whether we are working with a global energy giant, a growing charter school, or a local congregation, our mission remains the same: making technology easy, so you can focus on your mission.

Meet Our Leadership

GraceSoft’s leadership team combines hospitality experience with technology expertise to help lodging businesses operate more efficiently.

Gideon Stanley

CEO & Business Developer

In 1999, Gideon Stanley founded GraceSoft with a mission to simplify the digital landscape for the hospitality industry. While pursuing his Master’s in Software Engineering at the University of St. Thomas in St. Paul, MN, he worked closely with family friends Waldo and Marilyn Bunge at the Cottage House Inn in Lanesboro, MN. It was Waldo who challenged Gideon to create software that was truly accessible one that anyone, regardless of technical skill, could master. Gideon later went on to complete his MBA at Belhaven University, further bridging the gap between advanced technical architecture and strategic business management. 

Sarah Stanley

Director of Finance
