FAQ

Is GraceSoft’s Free Hotel Management Software really free?
  • Yes! GraceSoft’s Easy InnKeeping Lite is completely free for properties with fewer than 10 rooms.
  • There are no hidden fees or setup costs. You only pay a 1% fee after 250 Vrbo bookings per year—the PMS itself stays free forever.
What types of properties can use the free version?

Any small lodging property can use it — hotels, motels, inns, B&Bs, cabins, dude ranches, lodges, and vacation rentals — as long as the property has fewer than 10 rooms.

What features are included in the free version?
The free version includes:
  • A full reservation calendar
  • Our online booking engine Easy WebRez 4.0
  • Credit-card payments via Stripe
  • Editable email templates for reservation letters
  • Free Vrbo integration (up to 250 bookings)
  • Cloud access + mobile app (Android & iOS)
  • Email support
Is the software cloud-based and mobile-friendly?

Yes — it’s a cloud-based PMS, so there’s no installation or server required. You can access it anytime, anywhere, from a computer, tablet, or phone.
The Easy InnKeeping mobile app is available on both Android and iOS.

How does the free Vrbo integration work?

The free plan lets you connect to Vrbo and automatically sync bookings, rates, and availability — up to 250 bookings at no cost.
After that, only a 1% fee per booking applies, while the PMS remains free.

What kind of support do I get with the free plan?
  • You’ll get email-based support and access to our online help centre.
  • Paid plans include phone and live chat support, but for small properties, the email option is ideal for routine questions and setup help.
Can I upgrade later if I need more features or rooms?

Absolutely. You can upgrade anytime to Advanced, Premium, or Platinum plans when you grow or want features like channel management, advanced reports, or full multi-property control.
