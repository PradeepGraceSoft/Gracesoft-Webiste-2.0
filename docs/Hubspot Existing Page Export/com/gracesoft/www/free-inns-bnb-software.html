FAQ 

Need clarification?

What is B&B Booking Software?

B&B Booking Software is a specialized reservation system designed for bed and breakfasts. It allows property owners to manage room availability, handle guest bookings, process payments, and automate communication—all from a single, user-friendly dashboard. 

Who is this software designed for?

Our booking software is built for small-scale hospitality businesses like bed and breakfasts, boutique inns, guest houses, homestays, and vacation rentals. It's perfect for independent property owners looking to streamline reservations and boost direct bookings. 

Can I accept online bookings through my website?

Yes! Our B&B booking software includes a direct booking engine that integrates seamlessly with your website. Guests can check availability, choose rooms, and make secure payments in real-time. 

Does it sync with platforms like Airbnb, Booking.com, or Expedia?

Absolutely. Our software includes a built-in channel manager that automatically syncs bookings, rates, and availability across popular OTAs like Airbnb, Booking.com, and Expedia, reducing double bookings and saving you time. 

Is there a free trial available?

Yes, we offer a free [X-day] trial so you can explore all features of our B&B booking software. No credit card required—just sign up and start managing your reservations.
