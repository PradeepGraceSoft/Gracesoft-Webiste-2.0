Is Easy InnKeeping truly Easy?

Easy InnKeeping’s calendar grid view, also known as the Tape chart in a typical hotel software, provides the bird’s eye view of the entire hotel. At a glance you can see who is checked in, payment status, vacant rooms / room types, check-ins, check-outs and group bookings.Further customize your view by designating a color for each type of reservation status. Enter a reservation by right clicking, or control click for Mac users, on the grid. Process payments by clicking on the name of the guest in the room.When you right click or control click on the grid cell a menu pops up, and has everything that you will need to do for a reservation, from changing status to taking payments to adding new charges to their bill.

Analyze & Secure your data

Automate your Night audit operation

Night audit is one of the core functions of a hotel operation.  Night audit operations are fully automated as would be expected in any cloud based Hotel software. Using the options setting screen , specify the exact time when Night audit is to be run, closing the reservations that are checked out.  Several audit reports are generated after the night audit operation has run.

Secure your guest data

With more and more internet vulnerabilities, security has become a key component that differentiates novice hotel software vs. a proven company that can handle thousands of transactions and financial data in a secure environment.  The initial product release of Easy InnKeeping cloud dates back to 2006. Our servers are hosted in one the most secure level 4 data centers where other companies such as Boeing and highly secure companies host their servers. We are also PCI compliant, and run regular audits to stay abreast with the latest protocols.

Customize Employee Access

Easy InnKeeping allows users to give specific access limits and access rights, so any user can be allowed or denied access to a set of screens or specific functions in the software. For instance, the housekeeping staff could be giving access only to see the housekeeping module and reports.  This provides a way for managers to manage logins and passwords, shift timings, shift reporting and other key access levels based on an employee’s login profile.

Measure your Results, Track Progress

Choose from over 50 predefined reports to measure occupancy rates, daily income, or forecast future reservation activity. Customize dates, sort columns and export any of the reports to Excel for distribution to other departments and supervisors. Reports are available for every department such as Housekeeping, Sales, Front Desk, Night Audit and accounts.

Easy to Use for the Front Desk One page booking!

Easy InnKeeping Quick Res screen allows users to complete a reservation from one screen. Begin by entering the name of the guest. It it’s a repeat guest, matching guest profiles will pop up. When you select their name, all their existing data like address, email and payment information are pulled into the Quick Res screen. You can take payments, process a credit card and send an email to them right from this screen. This is a one page booking screen that makes the reservation process simple and fast. This screen is used for reservations that are very basic, like a single rate and a single unit.

Booking Packages

Travel or vacation packages are a great way for properties to promote value added services and amenities to their guests. The package feature allows a property to build combinations of room stays, and other items such as dinner for two, ski tickets or spa services or any other service that can be included into a package and sold as a single item to the guest.  After creating a package in Easy InnKeeping, guests can book directly through the Easy WebRez Package booking page on their web site, or staff can reserve a package booking using the Reservation > Package Booking screen. When the software records the data internally it is accounted as room revenue and revenue generated as a service individually thus hiding the actual cost of the items to the guests.

Online bookings for Groups

The Group Booking feature in Easy InnKeeping is ideal for blocking rooms for groups such as a wedding party, company gathering or corporate retreats.  The Group feature allows a hotel manager to block a set of rooms for a specific date range under the name of the group at a specific Group rate. The wedding party for instance can call the property and book the individual units based on the group rate negotiated by the group owner. Both individual and group payments are allowed from the Group Booking billing screen.

Each group has a group code assigned to it. Online group reservation makes this special rate options and group booking very simple and automatic.  The users of the group are emailed a unique group code, and individual users from the group can click on that link and enter the Group code.  This opens up the rooms only blocked for this group to be available to them at the specific rate assigned to them.

Monthly booking for Extended Stays

The monthly booking screen allows hotels that provide extended stay options an easy way to make monthly bookings. Tax exempt options for the extended stays and a simple signature capturing device to get electronically signed contracts are made available in the software. With a single click the number of months of stay can be increased or reduced.

Keeping in touch with Your Guests; Automatic Email

Enable automatic emails to your guests based on arrival, departure, anniversary or birthdays. Before a guest arrives it is recommended that you could send them a welcome email telling them about their upcoming trip and how the weather conditions are at the moment, directions to the place and offering them some kind of upgrades or amenities. Similar to a Welcome email, it is also a good practice to send a guest a Thank You email after one or two days from their check out date asking them for their feedback on their experience. This is done automatically by the software and pre-defined in the auto email settings. Sending auto emails saves countless hours of time consuming data entry and makes this guest connect easily with the hotel.

Email Marketing

Email marketing is still a great way to nurture your guest relationships, with periodic newsletters, promotions, packages and specials to entice your guest to consider returning to your property for their birthday, anniversary or other recreational occasions.  Easy Connect provides ways to customize your guest lists by categories such as type of guest or geographic location. Create email templates with your own logo, photos and graphics.

Yield Management

Price rooms based on supply and demand to maximize profits. If you manage rates by occupancy, this feature will enable to you to increase or decrease rates for a unit based on the number days left and based on the number of rooms left unreserved.   You can also automate the price changes according to date criteria.

Channel Manager

For some properties the higher fees charged by the GDS system, do not offer a good return on investment.  A channel manager is a lower fee option for connecting with a smaller list of online travel agents.  For the more unique or smaller property, a channel manager is a great way to list your property and sell rooms on the larger sites such as Expedia and Booking.com.  Easy InnKeeping provides two channel managers to select from.

Direct Connects

The biggest savings on online travel fees are using GraceSoft Direct Connects to Expedia, Booking.com, and Trip Advisor. Control your availability and rates in one location and receive reservations directly from Expedia, Booking.com, and Trip Advisor into the Easy InnKeeping PMS.

Sales & Account Management

Sales staff can use Easy Connect to manage their communications with corporate, conference and other sales accounts.  Schedule calls, create sales emails, log and track contact from initial inquiry to completing a booking contract.

Direct & Group Billing
