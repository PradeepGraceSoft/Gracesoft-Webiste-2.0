Frequently Asked Questions

What is GraceSoft Hospitality CRM?

GraceSoft Hospitality CRM is a hospitality-focused customer relationship management platform that helps properties manage guest profiles, loyalty programs, marketing campaigns, guest communications, feedback collection, and engagement activities from a centralized system.

How does the CRM work with GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping PMS?

The CRM integrates directly with GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping PMS, ensuring guest profiles, reservation information, loyalty activity, and communication history remain synchronized across both systems.

Does the CRM support multiple properties?

Yes. The CRM maintains unified guest profiles across multiple properties, providing complete visibility into guest activity and interactions throughout your organization.

Can guests manage their own information?

Yes. Guests can access the Guest Hub portal to update profiles, view reservations, make payments, purchase services, and manage their guest information.

Does the CRM include loyalty management?

Yes. The platform allows properties to create loyalty programs, award points, offer rewards, and encourage repeat bookings through personalized loyalty initiatives.

Can the CRM collect guest feedback?

Yes. Properties can create custom feedback forms and surveys that are automatically sent after guest stays to measure satisfaction and gather valuable insights.

How does the CRM prevent duplicate guest records?

The system validates guest profiles using email addresses to prevent duplicate records and maintain clean, accurate customer data.
