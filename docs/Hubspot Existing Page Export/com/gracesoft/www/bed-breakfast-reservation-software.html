What is GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping Bed and Breakfast Reservation Software?

GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping is a cloud-based bed and breakfast reservation software designed to help inns, B&Bs, lodges, cabins, and hotels manage reservations, guest communication, housekeeping, and online bookings from a single platform.

It combines a property management system (PMS), an online booking engine, email marketing, and a channel manager to simplify operations and increase direct bookings.

GraceSoft has been serving the hospitality industry since 1999, delivering reliable property management solutions to Bed and Breakfasts for over 25 years.
