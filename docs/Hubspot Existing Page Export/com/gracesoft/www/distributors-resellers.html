Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Houston, Texas, GraceSoft has designed and developed cost effective solutions for hotels, and any type of lodging facility in the hospitality industry. GraceSoft’s flagship product “Easy InnKeeping” demonstrates the key ingredients for a successful business!

The Houston-based corporation serves a suite of services, including a) property management software, b) point of sale for restaurants and gift shops, c) credit card processing, d) online booking, e) central reservation service, f) customer relationship software, g) web designing and search engine optimization. GraceSoft is well known in the hospitality industry and has received many accolades as the provider of Easy InnKeeping.

The Hospitality suite of products have been upgraded with the latest cloud technology. In 2005 Gracesoft introduced its first cloud based product for this market.
