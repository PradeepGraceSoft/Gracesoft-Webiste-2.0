About Me

Prakash Daniel is a global evangelist and businessman. He began his career in 1999 in the IT sector and has worked across various industries in India. Throughout Prakash’s career he managed database projects, created daily statements, secured shipments, insured quality control procedures were followed properly, and supervised vendor relationships.

In 2010, he expanded his mission by becoming a worldwide evangelist, focusing on marketplace ministry and addressing business networks and fellowships internationally. He is dedicated to connecting and empowering businessmen, businesswomen, and entrepreneurs.
