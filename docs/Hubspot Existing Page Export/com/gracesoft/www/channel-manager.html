What is a Hotel Channel Manager?

A hotel channel manager is software that helps hotels distribute room availability, pricing, and inventory across multiple online travel agencies (OTAs), such as Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, Google Hotel Search, and Vrbo, in real time. GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping Channel Manager automatically synchronizes all your listings, helping you avoid overbookings and increase global visibility. 

Why Choose GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping Channel Manager?

GraceSoft Easy Innkeeping Channel Manager is designed for modern hospitality businesses that want to simplify operations and increase bookings. 

With our channel manager, you can increase bookings across global OTA platforms, sync rates and inventory instantly, eliminate manual updates and double bookings, improve occupancy and revenue, and manage everything from one centralized dashboard.
