How GraceSoft’s Booking Engine & PMS Transforms Lodging Management

GraceSoft enhances Easy InnKeeping with an all-in-one Booking Engine and PMS designed to boost revenue, automate operations, and improve guest experience for resorts, inns, B&Bs, vacation rentals, and RV parks. It seamlessly integrates with major platforms like Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, Google, and Vrbo to increase visibility and drive more bookings across all key channels.

Eliminate Double Bookings with GraceSoft’s Seamless Vrbo Integration!

Some property management systems and channel managers rely on iCalendar to sync bookings from Vrbo. However, this method has slower updates and often requires manual data entry into your PMS, increasing the risk of double bookings. 

GraceSoft integrates directly with Vrbo, enabling real-time booking updates and minimizing double bookings for a seamless experience.
