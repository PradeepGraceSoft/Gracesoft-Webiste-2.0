Whether you are a Large property owner or own a smaller vacation rental, Easy InnKeeping vacation rental software perfectly fits your requirements to run your business seamlessly.

 

Short Term Rental Management Software

Our vacation rental software includes all the top features in our hotel reservation software. It is a one-stop-shop solution, comes with a separate reservation calendar where you can see the total number of reservations that happened, who has checked in & checked out, who has paid & more.

Streamlined operations from front desk to housekeeping, sync rates & availability in real-time with online booking engine & online travel agencies, and managed reservations in a single destination.

Confirmation emails & Text message

Easy reservation with confirmation text message & emails. After the guest books their slot, they will get an instant notification through emails & text message. There are plenty of pre-loaded templates available, so you can able to create confirmation emails & other marketing emails manually. Also, the software allows you to send bulk emails to the guests to know about the promotions & updates in your property for the future.

Multi-property feature

Are you own various rental properties in different locations & looking for a single software that needs to satisfy all your expectations to manage your reservations effortlessly, Easy InnKeeping vacation rental property management software is the best option to choose.

The multi-user feature lets you access the software by different persons with different login portals. This feature helps you create different dashboards for your employees, and also you can assign tasks for them separately there. Also, you can choose your preferred language.
