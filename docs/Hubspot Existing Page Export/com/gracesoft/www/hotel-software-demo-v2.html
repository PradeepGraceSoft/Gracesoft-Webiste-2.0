Easy WebRez 3.0 Booking Engine Demo

 

Increase online bookings with the latest design of Easy WebRez, the commission free booking engine! Make it easy for your return guests to book directly with your property with this mobile friendly design.

Showcase Your Property

Guests will be enticed with the first view of your booking page!  Easy WebRez 3.0 has the ability to showcase your property, by loading your photos as background images, along with high quality images of your rooms.

 

 

Easy to Book

Guest can immediately view all available rooms, pricing and Book Now!  Filter options are readily seen at the top of the page, so the guest can easily switch room selection, date selection and number of guests without leaving the page.

After selecting BOOK NOW button, the guest completes all payment details in one secure page.  Payments are securely processed through one of our PCI compliant providers, and immediate email confirmation is sent to the property and guest.

 

Get Go BNB

Every property that utilizes the Easy WebRez 3.0 Booking Engine will be eligible for a free listing on our partner directory, GetBoBNB.com.
