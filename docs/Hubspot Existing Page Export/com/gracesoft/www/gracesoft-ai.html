How GraceSoft AI Assistant Works

A simple, automated booking workflow designed for maximum efficiency and accuracy.

  1. Guest calls your property or starts a chat.
  2. Voice AI or Chat AI responds immediately.
  3. AI collects dates, preferences, and guest details.
  4. Availability and pricing are fetched in real time.
  5. A booking link is sent, or the reservation is completed over the call.
  6. Confirmation is sent automatically.
  7. All data syncs with the GraceSoft PMS and Booking Engine.

This ensures precision, speed, and a fully automated booking experience.

How does GraceSoft AI Voice Assistant help increase bookings?

GraceSoft’s AI Voice Assistant captures missed calls, handles booking queries 24/7, and reduces abandoned calls, helping properties gain more direct reservations. 

Does GraceSoft PMS include an online Booking Engine?

Yes. GraceSoft offers a commission-free booking engine that integrates with your website, supports promo codes, and syncs with the PMS.
