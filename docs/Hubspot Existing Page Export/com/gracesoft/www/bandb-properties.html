Canopy Tower Bed & Breakfast
 1 March 2015
Canopy Family has two award-winning birding lodges, the Canopy Tower, not far from Panama City in Soberanía National Park, and the Canopy Lodge, in the hills of El Valle de Antón. Both offer world-class, unique birding and natural history experiences, led by friendly, knowledgeable bilingual guides.
The Canopy Tower features rooms and an observation deck mere feet from the canopy of the rainforest, virtually at eye-level with such avian treats as toucans, tanagers, honeycreepers and hawks, as well as amazing looks at two- and three-toed sloths and Geoffroy’s Tamarins and Mantled Howlers, two monkey species otherwise nearly impossible to see this well!  From the Canopy Tower you can visit legendary Pipeline Road, arguably the best birding location in Mesoamerica, and other very birdy sites.

The South Glenora Tree Farm Bed & Breakfast
 1 April 2015
We welcome you to stay at The South Glenora Tree Farm B&B, featuring two converted barns built in 1855 and converted to a B&B in 1992. Relax in our tranquil country setting in the heart of the Finger Lakes. Centrally located between Seneca and Keuka Lakes. As pristine as the inside of our barns definitely are, one cannot stay too long without walking the property. Our B&B sits on 42 acres with eight-foot wide trails bordering Big Stream (which had 11 grist mills in its day). There are also trail cut meadows and the tree farm (Frasier, Douglas, and Scotch Pines), some archways for your leisure to walk through or sit a spell. During the winter, the trails are available for cross-country skiing or snow shoeing.
The peaceful covered wraparound porch offers 13 gliders to rock the evening away. There is also a covered pavilion on the property with picnic tables for you to use. In the morning, you’ll awaken to a full fabulous country breakfast which includes a fruit dish and a hot entrée served with juice, coffee, and assorted teas. In the evening, enjoy a complimentary snack in our comfortable great room. A well-stocked refrigerator with complimentary beverages is also available to all of our guests.
