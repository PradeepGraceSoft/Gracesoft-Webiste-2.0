Webinar Recap - Stay Cyber-Safe: Protect Your Property from Modern Threats

Hotels and B&Bs are now major targets for cyberattacks. Most breaches happen through simple mistakes—phishing emails, fake websites, weak passwords, and unsecured Wi-Fi networks. 

Presented by:
Dr. Johanna Jacob – PhD in Cybersecurity (UTSA), Faculty at Trinity University
Gideon Stanly – CEO, GraceSoft 

Key risks to watch for:

  • Phishing Attempts: Emails or messages pretending to be guests or vendors. 
  • Fake Websites: Pages copied to trick guests into giving payment details. 
  • Password Attacks: Hackers logging in using stolen or reused credentials. 
  • Unsecured Networks: Shared guest and office Wi-Fi increase vulnerability. 

Cyber Awareness Reminder:
Always verify messages, use strong passwords, keep networks separate, and educate your staff about common scams.
