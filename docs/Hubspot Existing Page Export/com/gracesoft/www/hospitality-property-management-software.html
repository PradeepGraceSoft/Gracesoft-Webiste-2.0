FAQ

Need clarification?

What is GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping?

Easy InnKeeping is cloud-based hospitality property management software that connects reservations, front desk operations, direct online bookings, OTA channels, housekeeping, payments, reporting, and guest management.

Who is GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping designed for?

It is designed for hotels, motels, inns, bed and breakfasts, boutique hotels, resorts, vacation rentals, cabins, lodges, campgrounds, RV parks, hostels, extended-stay properties, and multi-property businesses.

Can guests book directly from my website?

Yes. The integrated Booking Engine lets guests view availability, select accommodations, make secure payments, and receive instant confirmation directly from your website.

Does GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping connect with online travel agencies?

Yes. The Channel Manager connects with supported channels such as Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb, Vrbo, and Google Hotels to synchronize rates, availability, and reservations.

Does GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping support multiple properties?

 Yes. Easy InnKeeping supports both individual properties and growing multi-property operations. 

Is GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping cloud-based?

Yes. Authorized users can securely access the platform from any device with an internet connection.

Can GraceSoft help migrate my information?

Yes. Our implementation specialists can assist with transferring applicable reservation, guest, room, and property information from your existing system. 

Does GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping include AI guest communication?

 GraceSoft offers AI Voice and Chat Assistants that can answer guest questions, provide availability and rate information, and guide guests toward direct bookings 24/7.
