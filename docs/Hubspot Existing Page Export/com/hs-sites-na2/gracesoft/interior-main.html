All The Powerful Features You Expect...With Ease Of Use Your Staff Wants

EGraceSoft provides Easy to Use software solutions for hotel lodging properties that are comprehensive and accessible from anywhere. You will love the way Easy InnKeeping software integrates all critical operations into one single platform.

The Easy InnKeeping Hotel software is a "One Stop Shop" where you can manage all your bookings; from your front desk, call center, your own website booking engine, and OTA connections such as Expedia, Trip Advisor and Booking.com. All your bookings from multiple channels are managed in a single web based system. You can access Easy InnKeeping from any type of computer or tablet device with an internet connection.

  • Credit Card processing
  • Sales & Marketing Module , e-mail and marketing correspondence
  • Easy WebRez Online Reservation System
  • Global distribution system. Sell rooms through top online travel sites and travel agents
  • Google Analytics connects to your reservation software to bring the power for analytics to your booking engine.

The System where you Manage Reservations on One Screen

Easy to Use for the Front Desk

Tape Chart / Rooms View

The Easy InnKeeping room grid highlights reservations for 1 to 30 days at-a-glance. This makes blocking out rooms easy. Making a complex reservation is not only possible, but a breeze. Click on the calendar grid to select the room and date, then use a single screen to enter all your reservation data.

Analyze and Secure your Data

Security levels & Audit reports

Easily setup access levels based on employee logins. Allow or deny access to specific operations such as night audit.  50 predefined reports, custom reports, export to excel and nightly backup report sent to a designated email.

Choose your reservation style!

Flexible reservation screens

Easy InnKeeping Hotel Software offers 7 different reservation entry screens to suit a variety of property types. Quick Res for simple one page booking, Full Res for multiple rooms and dates of stay, Room Type Res, Package Res, Group Res and Monthly Res for long term stays.

Advanced Features keep You Connected, and Competitive

Sell amenities and conference services

Hourly reservation  / Conference room / Groups

Easy InnKeeping provides an online hourly booking feature for conference rooms, spas and other services. Group bookings are managed easily through a block rooms feature. Group billing allows guests to split the bill or pay as a group.

Keep in touch with Your Guests

Auto Email & Email Marketing, CRM

The auto email feature sends out any number of pre-defined emails during every step of the reservation process. The CRM (content management system) enables users to create email lists and send out bulk emails such as newsletters and promotions.

Maximize your profits!

Yield Management

Our tools help you price rooms based on supply and demand to maximize profits. The prices can be controlled by either date ranges or by occupancy. Create custom packages that can be sold online to guests.

Your gateway for Global Distribution

Booking engine/OTA/GDS & Channel Manager

The hotel software provides a way to market your property on your own website through the Easy WebRez booking engine. You can also market rooms with other OTAs such as Expedia, Booking.com, Trip Advisor and multiple channels through a two way interface that updates availability to sell, and also downloads bookings made.

Manage Sales & Corporate Accounts

Corporate accounts/ Direct billing

Setup corporate or government accounts and provide monthly invoicing options using direct billing. Monthly aging reports are generated and payment details can be easily tracked and emailed.  Sales staff can use Easy Connect to manage their communications with corporate, conference and other sales accounts.

Mobile Friendly!

Mobility & Tablets

Login directly to your Easy InnKeeping software through a mobile phone or tablet device. All hotel software features are optimized to work on the mobile devices just as it will work on a regular computer. Our engineers use the latest twitter boot strap technology to make mobile access work for you!

Seamless billing and POS

Easy WebPOS

Our point-of-sale system interfaces with the Hotel software to manage your gift shop and restaurant charges. The POS charges are automatically transferred to the room bill.
