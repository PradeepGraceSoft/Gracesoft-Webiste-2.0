# Content strategy playbook — GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping

**Use this file as the standing content brief for future GraceSoft website copy.** It captures the business goal, audience, message, source rules, page roles and conversion principles so they need not be re-explained for every new page.

**Owner goal:** Grow qualified organic and answer-engine visibility and turn relevant lodging-operator visits into demos, pricing conversations and other approved leads.

**Status:** Local editorial guidance. It does not authorize changes to HubSpot, published pages, forms, tracking, settings or redirects.
**Updated:** 2026-11-02, after reviewing the restored HubSpot export and current public pages.

## 1. Source hierarchy and review standard

Use sources in this order:

1. Current owner-approved product, commercial, legal and brand facts.
2. Current live GraceSoft product/solution pages and current published proof, treated as statements to verify rather than independent substantiation.
3. Restored HubSpot HTML export under `Hubspot Existing Page Export/`, treated as a historical snapshot of rendered pages and templates.
4. Competitor pages as references for content structure and buyer questions only. Never use competitors to substantiate GraceSoft claims.

The restored export is read-only reference material. `docs/export-page-inventory.csv` contains an automated title/H1/meta inventory of HTML files; `docs/export-analysis.md` summarizes page families, repeated content and review signals. Inventory metadata is a navigation aid, not proof of published status, page IDs, URL ownership, traffic, indexability or canonical target. The export README also warns that dynamic content may not render into static files.

For any new copy, record source URL/file and retrieval date for factual claims. If live copy and export differ, prefer current verified facts and note the difference. Do not infer that an exported filename is an active route. Recheck live destinations and redirects before release.

## 2. Brand and audience

**Product/vendor:** GraceSoft is the vendor; Easy InnKeeping is the product. Use `GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping` consistently until the owner confirms capitalization. The export and live site show variants (`Easy Innkeeping`, `Easy InnKeeping`, `Easy Innkepping`), so correct spelling in new copy and flag any brand decision for owner review.

**Core category:** hospitality / hotel property management software (hotel PMS). Define property management system (PMS) on first use when helpful. Define online travel agency (OTA) on first use.

**Buyer roles:** owners, general managers, front-desk teams and lodging operators.

**Property audiences present in the live navigation/export:** hotels and motels; B&Bs and inns; resorts; vacation rentals and serviced apartments; campgrounds/RV parks; condos; dude/guest ranches; and multi-property businesses. Keep these audiences reachable through descriptive links. Prioritize the best-supported/highest-value audience in a page's main message rather than cramming every audience into every heading.

**Core buyer question:** Can this system help a property like mine organize reservations and daily lodging operations, and does it fit my booking and guest-service needs?

## 3. Positioning and message

Lead with the product and the work it helps lodging teams do. State the target property audience clearly. Explain the operational scope in straightforward terms: reservations, front desk, availability, online booking, distribution, guest information/communication and related workflows where verified.

Do not rely on generic claims such as “all-in-one,” “best,” “#1,” “seamless,” “revolutionary,” “maximize revenue,” “eliminate double bookings” or “increase conversions” without precise evidence and legal/product approval. Prefer concrete tasks and links to the page that owns the detailed explanation.

### Recommended reusable message

**Short positioning:** GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping is hospitality property management software that helps lodging teams manage reservations and day-to-day operations, with connected options for online booking, channel distribution and guest communication.

**Qualification:** Available products, integrations and workflows may depend on the property's needs, location, plan and configuration. Confirm details with GraceSoft.

This is an editorial starting point; product and brand owners must verify exact capabilities and scope before publication.

## 4. Page roles and internal linking

Every page should serve one primary search/buyer intent and link to the next relevant decision. Keep the homepage as the product overview and route selector, not a duplicate of every solution page.

| Page family | Owns the detailed answer | Homepage role |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Who GraceSoft is, what Easy InnKeeping does, audiences served, product pathways and demo/pricing next steps | Concise product definition, core workflows, property-type links, proof and primary CTA |
| Hotel PMS / property management | Hotel and motel reservations, front desk, housekeeping, operations | Summary card |
| B&B / inn | Inn-specific reservations and workflows | Property-type link |
| Vacation rental | Short-term rentals, owners, portfolio and booking needs | Property-type link |
| Campground / RV | Sites, reservations and outdoor hospitality workflows | Property-type link |
| Condo / serviced apartment | Unit, owner, guest and maintenance workflows | Property-type link |
| Dude ranch | Lodging, packages, activities and ranch operations | Property-type link |
| Booking engine / websites | Direct web booking, website connection, visitor-to-reservation path | Capability card |
| Channel manager / integrations | Supported channels, sync behavior, setup and limits | Capability card; no long named partner list until verified |
| CRM / unified inbox | Guest profiles, messaging channels, follow-up and plan scope | Capability card |
| Payments / POS / devices | Payment providers, fees, security, hardware and configuration | Capability link; no gateway/security promises without evidence |
| AI | Voice/chat capability, availability, channels, configuration, limitations and price | Name as an option and link; avoid unverified outcome or uptime claims |
| Pricing | Current plans, fees, inclusions, add-ons and quote process | Link only; avoid stale prices in general copy |
| Demo / trial | What happens next, eligibility, form expectations and privacy | Primary demo action; trial is secondary only if current process is approved |
| Testimonials / case studies | Exact permissioned customer story and verified context | One carefully selected proof item with attribution and link |
| Blog / resources / support | Informational intent, maintained educational material and help | A small set of current useful resources, if it supports the buyer journey |

The export includes many temporary slugs, old alternatives, landing pages, blog copies, emails, templates, support/help routes and possible application pages. It is not a safe redirect/removal map. Check HubSpot status, actual URL, canonical, traffic, backlinks, forms, ownership and access before consolidation or migration.

## 5. Homepage content blueprint

Recommended sequence:

1. Header with clear product/property navigation, Pricing, Resources, Login and one visible `Request a Demo` CTA.
2. Hero naming GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping, the priority audience, one concrete operational benefit, approved product UI and two actions: `Request a Demo` (primary) and `View Pricing` (secondary).
3. Trust proof only when exact, current, permissioned and relevant.
4. Direct-answer definition of the product in crawlable HTML.
5. A concise set of workflow/outcome cards: reservations/front desk, direct booking, channel distribution, housekeeping/operations, guest communication, and payments/POS if still within the key offer.
6. Two or three real, current, rights-approved product screenshots with accurate alt text and captions.
7. Linked product capability cards; details belong on their dedicated pages.
8. Descriptive links for all supported property segments.
9. One permissioned customer quote or case study; omit the section if proof is not approved.
10. Five to seven source-checked FAQs answering common buyer questions.
11. Final demo CTA with a short explanation of what the visitor can expect.
12. Approved global footer and legal/contact/navigation details.

Avoid repeated “Get Started” links. Each link should describe its destination. Avoid duplicate homepage definitions, repeated feature grids, long unsupported “why choose us” copy and decorative elements that push the demo action far below the fold.

## 6. Search, AEO and copy standards

- Match real intents naturally: hotel property management software, hotel PMS, hotel reservation software, B&B/inn reservation software, vacation rental management, booking engine, channel manager and relevant property-specific needs. Use only where accurate; do not create a keyword checklist in prose.
- Use one descriptive page title, one meaningful H1, ordered H2/H3 headings and descriptive HTML links.
- Put direct answers near the relevant question. Keep important claims and FAQs visible in rendered text without interaction or client-side API fetch.
- Write for lodging operators. Use plain wording, short paragraphs, meaningful section labels and concrete tasks.
- Give one primary page each for pricing, demo, customer proof, integration details and each main property intent. Link to the detail page instead of copying it wholesale.
- Use metadata in the appropriate page settings. Verify canonical, indexability, social metadata and structured data in rendered staging later; do not assume an export's rewritten canonical is production-valid.
- Add schema only for verified entities and visible content; check for global/legacy duplicate nodes first. Never use ratings/reviews, awards, FAQ schema or software details as unsupported decoration.
- Google says its AI search features follow standard SEO foundations and add no special markup/files requirement. Helpful, accurate content, crawling, internal links, page experience and text availability matter; no inclusion or ranking is guaranteed.
- Review Search Console query/page impressions and clicks after publication, then compare qualified leads, demo completions and pricing actions to an agreed baseline. Never claim SEO, AEO, speed or conversion improvement without measurement.

## 7. Lead conversion standards

- Primary conversion: existing Request a Demo journey at `https://www.gracesoft.com/livedemo`, subject to a final route/form check.
- Secondary conversion: Pricing at `https://www.gracesoft.com/pricing`; verify its current page and commercial details before using it as a strong CTA.
- Keep CTA wording consistent and specific. Explain what happens after clicking only if the current demo process confirms it.
- Place the primary action in the header and hero, and repeat it near the end. Use mobile-friendly placement that does not obscure content.
- Use approved screenshots and customer proof close to decision points. Avoid fake urgency, invented guarantees, dummy forms or frictionless claims that aren't true.
- Keep analytics/event tracking changes in the approved remote-change process. No live tracking changes in local content work.

## 8. Claims and evidence gate

Before publishing a claim, ask: what exactly is promised, for whom, on which plan/region/configuration, as of what date, and what primary evidence proves it? Add the exact claim, source URL/file, retrieval date, verifier, scope, approval and handling to `docs/claims-review.md`.

The export and current pages contain claims that need careful validation, including:

- 25+ years / since 1999 / 27+ years; resolve dates and wording against an approved company record.
- 2,000+ customers/properties and other customer counts.
- SoftwareSuggest Top 13, Capterra rankings, awards, “best/#1” and review scores; establish source, date, eligibility and permission.
- “24/7” customer support vs AI inquiry handling; these are different promises and must not be conflated.
- Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, Vrbo, Google Hotels, TripAdvisor and any named integration; verify current connection mode, availability, geography, plan and partner/logo permission.
- Real-time synchronization, no double booking, commission-free bookings, direct revenue, conversion, occupancy, time savings, security, PCI compliance and payment provider claims.
- AI availability, voice/chat channels, live PMS access, rate/availability answers, booking completion, support hours and performance promises.
- Customer quotes, names, property affiliations, case study metrics and images; confirm exact wording, date and written permission.
- Cloud, mobile app, device, multi-property and feature/plan availability.

The fact that a claim appears in a public page, export or competitor page is not independent verification. Omit or qualify claims that lack evidence.

## 9. Competitor takeaways (structure only)

Reviewed for content patterns: Cloudbeds, Little Hotelier and Mews. They make the category/product clear, group capabilities under human-readable labels, show product UI or customer proof, answer category questions and make a demo action easy to find. Apply those patterns to GraceSoft's verified product story. Do not borrow their metrics, awards, scale claims, exact copy, integrations or comparative statements.

## 10. Reusable brief for a future writing request

When asked to draft or revise a GraceSoft page, use this playbook and `docs/claims-review.md`, then:

1. Identify the page's buyer intent, audience and one primary conversion.
2. Review the matching current live page and relevant export files in read-only mode; use `docs/export-page-inventory.csv` to locate candidates.
3. Confirm current routes and distinguish active public pages from export-only variants.
4. Draft direct, useful copy with descriptive internal links and page-appropriate CTA.
5. Mark claims, quotes, screenshots, awards, prices and data that need product, legal, brand or customer approval.
6. Add or update a dated local Markdown content artifact and link it from `README.md` or this playbook when it should be reused.
7. Do not edit exported reference files or make remote HubSpot changes unless a separately scoped explicit approval authorizes them.

Suggested request: “Draft [page/section] for [audience and goal]. Follow `docs/content-strategy-playbook.md`, inspect the matching live/export sources, and deliver local review copy plus an evidence/approval checklist. Do not publish or modify HubSpot.”
