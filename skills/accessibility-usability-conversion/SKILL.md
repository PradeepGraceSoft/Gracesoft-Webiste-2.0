# Accessibility, usability and conversion standards

Use during page design, implementation and review.

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Verify contrast, visible focus, keyboard operation, landmarks, heading order, labels and meaningful image alternatives.
- Provide a skip link. Make menus work with keyboard and Escape. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep core content and links usable without JavaScript. Avoid autoplay and hover-only actions.
- Test 320 CSS-pixel width, 200% and 400% zoom, portrait and landscape, keyboard-only navigation and screen reader basics.
- Use responsive image dimensions and lazy-load below-the-fold media. Do not lazy-load the LCP image.
- Make primary CTA clear and persistent without blocking content. Keep secondary actions visually quieter.
- Use real product screenshots and permissioned testimonials only. Do not invent outcome metrics, awards or review counts.
- Measure accessibility and usability findings. Do not report unrun checks as passing.
- Track demo, pricing and feature click events as a future specification only until analytics changes are approved.
