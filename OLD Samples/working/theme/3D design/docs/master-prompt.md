Act as a world-class creative director, senior UI/UX designer, 3D web developer, frontend architect, SEO strategist, and conversion optimization specialist.

Your task is to redesign and build a world-class website for **GraceSoft – Easy Innkeeping**, a hotel and hospitality property management software company.

Website: [https://www.gracesoft.com/](https://www.gracesoft.com/)
