Frequently asked questions

What is hospitality property management software?

A hospitality property management system (PMS) helps lodging teams organize reservations, room availability, guest records, billing and daily operations.

What is GraceSoft Easy InnKeeping?

Easy InnKeeping is GraceSoft’s cloud-based hospitality PMS, with connected booking, distribution, guest engagement and operational tools.

What types of properties does GraceSoft support?

GraceSoft serves independent hotels, motels, bed and breakfasts, inns, resorts, vacation rentals, campgrounds, RV parks, dude ranches, condos and multi-property businesses.

Can guests book directly through my website?

Yes. The Easy InnKeeping booking engine lets guests check availability and reserve directly through your website.

Explore the booking engine

Will it work with my booking channels and payment provider?

GraceSoft lists channels including Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb and Vrbo, and payment providers including Stripe, Square, Authorize.Net and Shift4. Ask GraceSoft to confirm support for your channels and provider in your region and configuration.

Review booking channels · Check payment integrations

Can GraceSoft manage multiple properties?

Yes. GraceSoft offers multi-property management, including access to multiple properties through a unified login. Discuss portfolio requirements with the team.

Does GraceSoft offer AI Voice and Chat Assistants?

Yes. GraceSoft describes AI voice and chat options for property questions, availability, rates and booking inquiries, including outside normal desk hours.

Does GraceSoft provide mobile access?

Yes. GraceSoft describes cloud access from desktop, tablet and mobile, with apps for Android, iPhone and iPad. Confirm the workflows available on your device.

How much does Easy InnKeeping cost?

Visit the GraceSoft pricing page to compare options. If you also need Website Builder, ask which services and fees apply.

Can I see the software before deciding?

Yes. Request a demo and tell GraceSoft which workflows you want to see, such as arrivals, direct bookings or guest messaging.

Can I bring over existing reservations and guest records?

Tell GraceSoft which system you use and what records you need to move. Ask which imports are supported and who will handle each step before setting a switch date.

What support is available?

Ask GraceSoft which support channels, hours and onboarding assistance apply to your plan.

Who can update my website?

If you are considering a website package, confirm who can edit room descriptions, photos and policies, and how to request changes.
